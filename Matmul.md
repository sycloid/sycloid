Resources on implementing matrix multiplication on GPU

- Amanzhol Salykov: "[Advanced Matrix Multiplication Optimization on NVIDIA GPUs](https://salykova.github.com/sgemm-gpu)"
- Colfax Research: "[Categorical foundation of CuTe layouts](https://research.colfax-intl.com/categorical-foundations-for-cute-layouts)"
